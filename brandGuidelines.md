# Paper and Peach Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name
==Paper and Peach==

Always written with the word "and" spelled out. Never written with an ampersand (&) or shortened.

### Acceptable Usage
- Paper and Peach
- PAPER AND PEACH (all caps)

### Unacceptable Usage
- Paper & Peach (ampersand)
- Paper n Peach (shortened)
- paper and peach (all lowercase)
- PaperandPeach (no space)
- Paper and Peaches (plural)

---

## Logo Usage

### Clear Space
Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the peach icon on all sides.

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 120px width minimum
- Print: 1 inch / 25mm width minimum

### Logo Don'ts
- Do not stretch or distort the logo
- Do not rotate the logo
- Do not change the logo colors outside of approved variations
- Do not add effects (glows, gradients, heavy shadows)
- Do not place the logo on busy backgrounds

## Typography

### Heading Font
==Fredoka==
- Weights: SemiBold (600), Bold (700)
- Fallback: Baloo 2, system-ui, sans-serif

Use as: Headings, buttons, countdown numbers in the photobooth

### Body Font
==Nunito==
- Weights: Regular (400), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)
- Fallback: Inter, system-ui, sans-serif

Use as: Body text, forms, navigation, product descriptions

### Accent Font
==Caveat==
- Weight: Regular (400)

Use as: Short handwritten notes and sticker labels only. Never for paragraphs.

### Type Scale (Mobile / Desktop)
- Display: 36px / 56px
- H1: 30px / 44px
- H2: 24px / 32px
- H3: 20px / 24px
- Body: 16px / 16px
- Small: 14px / 14px

### Typography Rules
- Use sentence case for headings ("Start designing")
- Keep body line length between 45 and 75 characters
- Minimum body text size: 16px on mobile

## Shape and Style

### Corner Radius
- Buttons and tags: pill (999px)
- Inputs: 16px
- Cards: 24px
- Modals: 28px

### Sticker Effect
- White outline: 4–6px solid #FFFFFF
- Soft shadow: 0 6px 16px rgba(74, 44, 42, 0.15)
- Optional slight rotation between -3° and +3°

### Paper Texture
- Subtle paper-grain overlay at 3–6% opacity on page backgrounds
- Do not apply over text-heavy areas or the design canvas

## Imagery

### Photography Style
- Natural, soft daylight with warm tones
- Props: paper, washi tape, notebooks, tote bags, plants
- Backgrounds: cream, kraft paper, pastel solids

### Photography Don'ts
- No cold, blue-toned, or overly corporate stock photos
- No harsh flash or heavy shadows
- No cluttered backgrounds that compete with the product

### Icons
- Rounded line icons, 2px stroke, 24 × 24px grid
- Default color: Cocoa Brown; active state: Peach Deep

---

## Voice and Tone

### Brand Voice
- Friendly, like a creative friend
- Simple and clear, no jargon
- Playful, but never silly
- Reassuring, especially around uploads and payments

### Writing Rules
- Use "you" and "we"
- Default language is Indonesian, casual-polite (use "kamu")
- Use emojis sparingly (maximum 1–2 per message): 🍑 ✨ 💌 📸
- Never blame the user
- Never show technical error codes to users
- Buttons use action verbs: "Start designing," "Add to cart"

### Sample Microcopy
- Primary CTA: Mulai Desain / Start Designing
- Empty cart: Keranjangmu masih kosong 🍑 / Your cart is empty 🍑
- Camera permission: "May we borrow your camera? Photos stay on your device unless you choose to upload."
- Order success: Yay! Pesananmu sudah kami terima. / Yay! We've received your order.

## UI Components

### Buttons
- Primary: Peach fill, Cocoa text, pill shape; hover changes to Peach Deep
- Secondary: Paper White fill, 2px Cocoa border, Cocoa text
- Danger: Berry fill, white text
- Minimum touch target: 44 × 44px

### Inputs
- 16px radius, 1.5px Border Warm outline, Paper White fill
- Focus: Peach Deep border
- Labels always visible above fields

### Badges
- New: Butter
- Best seller: Blush
- Eco: Mint

---

## Design Studio Guides

- Cut line: dashed Berry (#D6455D)
- Bleed area: translucent Berry fill
- Safe zone: dashed Mint Deep (#3F9C7C)
- Print resolution: 300 DPI minimum
- Pin bleed: 3mm; sticker bleed: 2mm

---

## Photobooth Style

- Camera frame: 28px radius with white sticker-style border
- Countdown: Fredoka, oversized, Peach with white outline
- Filters: Warm Peach, Black and White, Vintage, Soft Pastel
- Privacy note (always visible near camera): "Your photos stay on your device unless you choose to upload them."

---

## Motion

- Micro-interactions: 150–200ms; transitions: 250–350ms
- Playful easing: cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion by disabling bouncing and confetti
